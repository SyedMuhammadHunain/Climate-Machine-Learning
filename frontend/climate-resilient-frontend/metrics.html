1. Model comparison

Model Val R² Val MAE Test R² Test MAE Test RMSE Test skill vs crop mean CV log-MAE
  • Val, Test: validation years and test years.
  • R²: share of the yield variation the model explains; higher is better.
  • MAE: average error in dataset yield units; lower is better.
  • RMSE: like MAE, but punishes large misses more.
  • Test skill vs crop mean: gain over the crop-province mean on a log scale; above 0 is better.
  • CV log-MAE: average error on a log scale across the cross-validation folds; lower is better.
