3. Dynamic Agroclimatic Elasticity Equations

Yield predictions combine non-linear Random Forest feature mappings with biological response functions to guarantee physical realism under extreme climate anomalies:

1. Moisture Deficit & Water Response Factor:
Deficit = max(0.0, (P_ref - P_sum) / P_ref)
W_factor = 1.0 - Kw * min(1.0, Deficit) [when P_sum < P_ref]
W_factor = 1.0 + 0.15 * min(1.0, (P_sum - P_ref) / P_ref) [when P_sum >= P_ref]
2. Thermal Stress Factor:
H_factor = max(0.25, 1.0 - 0.035 * (T_max - T_crit)) [when T_max > T_crit]
H_factor = 1.0 [otherwise]

3. Combined Agroclimatic Elasticity:
Elasticity = clip(W_factor * H_factor, min=0.10, max=1.50)
Final_Yield = round(clip(RF_pred * Elasticity, min=0.0), 2)
4. Drought Resilience Ranking Score:
w_prod = clip(1.0 - Deficit, 0.08, 0.92)
Drought_Resilience = Elasticity * (1.0 - Deficit * Kw)
Normalized_Yield = Predicted_Yield / max(Resilient_Yields)
Score = (1.0 - w_prod) * Drought_Resilience + w_prod * Normalized_Yield
